## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Stemix documentation site (operated by
Ourchitecture) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit
[stemix.dev](https://stemix.dev). By using this site, you agree to the
practices described here.

**Effective date:** March 31, 2026

---

## What We Collect

### Usage Analytics

We collect anonymized information about how visitors interact with this site,
including:

- Pages visited and navigation paths
- Approximate geographic region (country or region level — not city or street)
- Browser type, device category, and operating system
- Referring website or search query
- Time spent on pages and scroll depth

This data is collected in aggregated, anonymized form and is not linked to any
personally identifiable information.

### Session Behavior Data

We collect data about how visitors interact with page content — including
general click patterns and interaction sequences — to understand which content
is most useful and to identify areas for improvement. This data is processed in
aggregate and is not used to build individual user profiles.

### Cookies and Local Storage

This site uses the following:

| Type | Purpose | Duration |
| ---- | -------- | -------- |
| Analytics cookies | Anonymized visit tracking across sessions by third-party analytics services | Up to 14 months |
| Session insight cookies | Page interaction data collected by session recording services | Session or up to 1 year |
| Preference storage (local storage) | Remembers your cookie consent choice | Until cleared by you |

No advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

---

## How We Use This Information

Collected data is used exclusively to:

- Understand how visitors use the site and which content is most valuable
- Identify technical issues and improve site performance
- Inform documentation improvements and content prioritization

We do not sell, share, or transfer usage data to third parties for commercial
or advertising purposes.

---

## Third-Party Services

We use third-party analytics and session insight services to collect and process
usage data as described above. These services operate under their own privacy
policies and data processing agreements. We have configured these services to:

- Anonymize IP addresses before storage
- Limit data retention to what is necessary for site improvement purposes
- Disable data sharing with advertising networks

---

## Data Retention

Analytics data is retained for a maximum of 14 months before automatic
deletion, in line with industry best practices for web analytics.

## Your Choices

### Cookie Consent

When you first visit this site, you will see a notice asking whether you accept
analytics cookies. You can:

- **Accept** — analytics and session insight data will be collected as described.
- **Decline** — analytics tracking will be disabled for your browser session and
  this preference will be remembered.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by clearing your browser's
local storage and cookies for this domain, then reloading the page.

### Opt-Out Options

You may also choose not to be tracked by:

- Using your browser's private or incognito mode
- Enabling the Do Not Track setting in your browser
- Using a browser extension that blocks analytics scripts

### Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your data,
including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of information
associated with you. Because we collect minimal data and do not link it to
personal identities, there is typically no individually identifiable record to
retrieve or delete.

To raise any privacy concern, please open an issue in the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/ourchitecture/idp/issues).

data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored by third-party providers under their
respective security practices.
